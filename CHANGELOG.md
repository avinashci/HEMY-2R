## v2.2: November 2026

- **Dovetail bay:** closed side bay with a 10 A accessory jack, LED, and a USB-C serial module in a red-anodized insert; flush bay cover.
- **GPS restored** in a lid pocket under a non-metallic sticker.
- **Base:** single 10 A input jack and LED in one bay of the flange; base plate recess sized to the measured LA-40B top plate (56.5 × 56.5).
- **Side plate A:** USB-C window to the board's own USB-C; CN1 and DC1 left off the board; inner connector relief removed.
- **Home marks** on side A only.
- **Lid:** handle moved in line with the USB-C window, countersunk from below.
- **Torque table** for lubricated threads; drawing notes for anodize, tolerances and minimum walls.
- **HBK17 reducer measured:** matches the seller's CAD.
