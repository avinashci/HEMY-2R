# HEMY 2R (HEMY v2 restyle): v2.2

Based on [HEMY](https://github.com/polvinc/HEMY) by polvinc, GPL-3.0.

Current design (as of 2026-11-07). A HEMY v2 harmonic mount in a 125 mm cube shell with a SAL-33-style exterior: one-piece L-plate frame, 6 mm panels, M4 corner cubes, a lid handle, and an optional counterweight cover. Internals (reducers, motors, pulleys, belts, board) are stock HEMY v2; only the shell, frame and a few positions change.

## 3. Design decisions
- **125 mm cube, one-piece L-plate.** The RA reducer is moved 6 mm toward the back wall: shorter payload arm than v2.1's +15 mm, more room behind the RA motor, and the RA axis ends up almost centred under the cube.
- **Board turned 90°** so its edge connectors face side plate A; it sits under the lid at X −17…35. Board spacers move with it.
- **No external USB-C.** Flash or rescue with the lid off: the board hangs from the lid, so lift the lid and the USB-C is reachable. The StellaVita link is the dovetail USB-C (CH340, 3 wires to CN1).
- **12 mm M4 cubes in all 8 corners**, so every panel and lid screw is M4. Cubes are cheap and sacrificial (a worn thread costs a cube, not a panel) and keep the panels plain 6 mm plates.
- **Handle kept** (clears the board by 5.2 mm).
- **GPS window in the lid** at the back-centre (X −40, Y 0), behind the handle grip: one pocket **25.5 × 25.5 × 5 mm (R1)** with a Ø4 lead hole at its inner corner. A **25 × 25 × 4 passive ceramic GPS patch** (U.FL lead to the board's JP1) sits on a **1 mm double-sided foam pad**, so its top is flush with the lid, under the **"HEMY 2R" sticker (locked 2026-11-08: gear version with the credit line "based on HEMY by polvinc", 40 × 32 mm, R3, red #D62A2A with white artwork; `logo/HEMY_2R_GPS_sticker_PRINT.svg`, 1.5 mm bleed, magenta cut line, all text outlined)**, long side along the handle direction, overlapping the pocket 3.25 mm on the sides and 7.25 mm on the ends to seal out dew. **Printed** die-cut vinyl with a laminate, **non-metallic** (no chrome or foil). The lid faces the celestial pole, and the telescope only shades part of its sky; GPS is only needed for the first fix at start-up. The sticker replaces the separate lid banner.
- **Base flange v2.2, 80 × 80 × 22 mm** (grown from the Nov-2 70 × 70 concept so the 10 A jacks fit; same height, ~259 g): reducer interface copied from A11 (12 × M3 at Ø54, counterbored from below; the empty positions at 0/90/180/270° match the K17 drawing's four larger features); 60.2 × 60.2 × 3 mm recess (R4.1) and 4 × M6 × 9 deep at (±17.5, 0) / (0, ±17.5) from the RA axis for the LA-40B (plate **assumed** 60 × 60 × 10). **Side B face, one row:** LED – **MOUNT** – RA home mark – **ACC** – LED.
  - **Jacks:** 10 A 5.5 × 2.1 panel sockets (M12 thread 9 mm + body 9 mm, Ø14 bezel, 18 AWG leads, tethered cap), **screwed into tapped M12 holes** in the 5 mm wall (no nut), X ±15 from the axis, Z −21. LEDs Ø3.1 at X ±27.
  - **Bays** for the jack bodies and LEDs, machined from below (through the skirt on the inside of the face); 5 mm roof. A 6 mm slot from each bay to the Ø26 centre cavity, then up the reducer's **Ø19 H7 through-bore** (K17 drawing).
  - Side B is the only face with a screw-free sector wide enough (the far-wall and side-A faces have reducer screws 18° either side); the 18 mm jack depth is why the flange grew to 80 mm.
  - **Checked:** no clash with any v2.2 RA part (the one 82 mm³ overlap with a small disc inside the reducer pocket is in polvinc's model and the stock A11 has it too); jacks ≥ 2.0 mm from the reducer, 0.5 mm above the (assumed) LA-40B plate; thinnest walls 2.6 mm (reducer screw counterbore) and 3.2 mm (M6 thread).
- **No TMC5160 driver heatsinks** (not needed at 0.8–0.9 A): ~13 mm above the RA motor instead of 1.2 mm.
- **Weight:** about 4.6 kg without the base (stock HEMY < 4 kg; the 6 mm shell adds ~0.6 kg), ~5.6 kg with the LA-40B (808 g) and the base flange.

## 4. Optional counterweight cover
A swappable far wall for using a counterweight; the plain far wall is the everyday cover. HEMY carries 15 kg without one, so this is optional (heavier loads, a piggyback camera, less RA drive torque).
- **Socket:** SAL-33-style lug outside, 32 × 24 mm, R4, 12 mm proud, 1 mm chamfer, on the DEC axis (Y 0, Z 70). **M12 × 1.75** through lug and plate (~18 mm of thread), tapped by JLCCNC (no Helicoil).
- **Load path:** foot inside along the bottom edge (X 40–50.5, Y ±40, Z 9–19), held by **3 × M5 countersunk screws from under the floor** (X 45, Y −28 / 0 / +28) into M5 threads (8 deep) in the foot, plus the 4 M4 corners. **Swap:** 4 corner screws + 3 floor screws.
- **Bar and weight:** Angeleyes stainless bar Ø20 × 164 mm (M12; confirm the thread with the seller) and the 5 kg (11 lb) weight; centre ~7 cm beyond the far wall. With the D610 + 50 mm piggyback (~1.4 kg), ~12–13 cm out (a 314 mm bar gives room). Fender washer Ø35–40 under the toe saver (the weight's bore is larger than the cap).
- **Balance:** a harmonic drive can't be checked by swinging. Find the scope's balance point on a rod, weigh it, and match weight × distance about the RA axis; within 10–20% is fine. The counterweight lowers RA drive torque and cancels the moment on the RA bearing.
- **Build options (decide on cost):** (1) one piece from a ~29 mm block, as modelled; (2) three parts, HEMY v1.1 C10 style: 6 mm plate + bolt-on lug block (32 × 24 × 15, M12, 4 × M5 from inside) + foot bar (10 × 10 × 80, 3 × M5 from below, 2 small countersunk screws through the plate).
- Load ratings: LA-40B 40 kg, EVUMO tripod 40 kg; the full stack with counterweight is ~16 kg.

## 5. Home-position index marks (all on side B)
90° V-grooves, 0.3 mm deep, 8 mm long, cut in setups that happen anyway. Fill with white engraving crayon (e.g. Lacquer-Stik) or a white paint pen; they show well under a red headlamp.
- **RA:** side plate B, bottom border of the outer face, vertical, in line with the RA axis (X −6). Its partner is a matching V-groove on the **base flange's side B face** at X −6, 8 mm down from the top edge, between the two power jacks; with the mount in home position the two lines form one line.
- **DEC:** side plate B, horizontal at the DEC axis height (Z 70), running 8 mm in from the back edge, plus a **matching cut on the dovetail's side B face** at Z 70 (inner end, next to the body, X −88.5…−80.5), between the two clamp knobs. They line up when the scope is parallel to the RA axis.
- Side A, the lid and the dovetail's side A (USB pocket) carry no marks.

## 6. Hardware (screws)
Flat-heads to match the 90° countersinks: **ISO 14581 (Torx), stainless A2-70** (or A4-80). Torx grips better than hex on small flat-heads. No threadlocker on panels; a little anti-seize on the lid screws. Buy spares.

## 7. Build and assembly notes
- **Reducers:** RA reducer into the floor first, then slide the DEC reducer into the back wall. Clamp screws from outside (countersunk heads), hex nuts inside, only at the 10 holes with a matching frame hole; the counterbored holes on the reducer face are its own factory screws, so leave them.
- **Corner screws:** M4 × 10 everywhere, so the three screws meeting in one cube stop short of each other.
- **Brake stack, from day one:** fit both brake supports, the 5 × 40 shaft on two 625ZZ bearings, the 16T pulley and the **200 mm** RA belt over all three pulleys; set belt tension once. Until the SWB-003 (24 V) is fitted, leave the **coupling (part 13) off** so the 16T pulley idles; adding the brake later is bolt-on + coupling + plug into the brake board.
- **Brake board:** plugs onto the main board's BRAKE header (lid side). On first power-up, **measure ≈ 23 V at its output before connecting the brake**.
- **Dovetail CH340:** before wiring, plug it into USB and measure **TXD to GND: it must read ≈ 3.3 V** (the Teensy is 3.3 V only). Wire TXD → board RX, RXD → board TX, GND → GND (3 wires to CN1).
- **Power switch (TechEss 16 mm latching):** find the contact pair that closes when latched (multimeter), wire it to the PS1 pads (VIN and EN), insulate the other wires. Fit it to the lid and hand-tighten the 19 mm nut before the lid goes on.
- **Lid:** take it off to flash the board; the board comes up with it.
- **Wiring:** turning the board moved MOT1/MOT2, BRAKE, IN1 and CN1, so the wire lengths in `docs/` (v2.0 lid-tray wiring layout) are out of date. Measure on the bench and leave slack loops for RA/DEC rotation and for lifting the lid.
- **Desiccant:** put a small silica-gel pack inside the body against dew; swap or dry it now and then.
- **Firmware (OnStepX):** time and location from **GPS** (the lid has a GPS window now; the old "GPS off" note no longer applies); Serial1 / CN1 at **115200 baud** for the dovetail CH340 link; motor current 0.8–0.9 A.
- **Index marks:** home position = counterweight side down, scope parallel to the RA axis; the side B plate line and the base flange groove line up.
- **Base flange wiring:** screw the jacks into the tapped holes with a drop of medium threadlocker (the tethered cap's ring goes under the bezel). MOUNT and ACC run from the jacks through the slots and the centre cavity, up the reducer's Ø19 bore (which is the spinning input shaft). Run the wires through a thin PTFE or nylon sleeve fixed at the base so the shaft can't rub them, and leave a slack loop inside the body for RA rotation. ACC carries everything at the scope (the StellaVita is a 12 V / 8 A power hub): 18 AWG all the way, 10 A inline fuse at the Jackery. LEDs: each straight across its jack (+12 V / GND) with ~1 kΩ, or a 12 V LED with a built-in resistor; dim red.

## 8. Checks done (2026-11-06/07)
- Every M4 hole in the far walls, side plates and lid lands on a cube.
- Turned board (bounding boxes; real gaps equal or larger): corner cube 3.0 mm, DEC motor 1.3 mm, side-A connectors inside the relief (1.4 mm to its floor), switch 6.7 mm, handle 5.2 mm.
- Reducer insertion: RA first, then DEC; ≥1.0 mm clearance along the whole DEC slide with the 6 mm RA shift (7 mm would touch).
- After the RA shift: RA motor to far wall 8.2 mm, to back wall 15.5 mm; reducer to reducer 1.0 mm (as stock).
- TechEss switch envelope: 3.6 mm to the DEC motor, 7 mm to the board.
- The CAD's stray block on the DEC reducer (15° off the bottom) was removed; in the real reducer that spot is a recessed factory screw.

## 9. Open questions
- **Lid Ø10 features at (−57, ±44):** probably the rounded ends of a slot in the lid's underside (Z 119–122, X −69…−60) that the L-plate back wall's top (Z 122) tucks into. Confirm in Onshape, then close.
- **Brake board fit:** not in the model. It turns with the main board; on paper it now sits at about X 19–34, Y −46…−27, up to Z 115, clear of the switch, the GPS pocket and the corner cube (~4 mm). Check against real geometry (model it or measure on the bench).
- **Dovetail 12 V (ACC) output:** same 10 A M12 jack as the base (18 mm deep), next to the CH340 USB-C on the dovetail's side A. Not modelled yet; check its depth fits.
- **Base flange jacks:** confirm the M12 thread pitch on the real jack (nut) and its body length (drawing: 9 + 9 mm, Ø14 bezel) before ordering.
- **LA-40B top plate** (`reference/LA-40B interface drawing (Angeleyes).png`): 8 × M6 **threaded** on a 38 × 38 grid (corners ±19, sides ±17.5) + ¼-20 centre. With the base plate (section 10) these threads are used as designed: countersunk M6 from above, no drilling. Outline not dimensioned: scaled from the drawing it may be ~54 × 50 mm R4, not the assumed 60 × 60, so the recess waits for measurements: length, width, corner radius, thickness, anything raised on top, long side vs the latitude hinge, and hex-key access to the side holes from below. Base-plate screws into the LA-40B: countersunk M6, length = base plate floor + thread engagement (≤ LA-40B plate thickness); ~5 N·m, blue threadlocker.
