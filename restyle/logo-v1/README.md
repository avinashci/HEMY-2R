# HEMY v2 logo: cut-vinyl files (v1, 2026-11-02)
Two-colour cut vinyl: **layer 1 BLACK**, **layer 2 RED**. Everything else is bare bead-blast silver (the letters "HEMY", screw heads, pinstripe and highlights are knock-outs). All paths are filled outlines (no strokes, no fonts) at 1:1 mm with a 2 mm margin.

| Sticker | Art size | Where |
|---|---|---|
| HEMYv2_sideplate_logo_90mm_* | 90.0 × 67.1 mm | Side plate(s) (110 × 125 mm), centred |
| HEMYv2_lid_banner_46mm_* | 46.0 × 8.6 mm | Lid top, centred 21 mm right of centre (between handle and edge) |

Files per sticker: `_layer1_BLACK.svg`, `_layer2_RED.svg` (cut files, layers don't overlap), `_both_layers.svg` (for a shop that does both colours from one file), `_preview_on_silver.svg/.png` (look only).
Colours: black #141414, red #D62A2A (pick the vendor's nearest gloss/matte vinyl; matte black suits bead blast).
Fonts used for the outlines: Bungee (OFL).
Apply: clean with isopropyl alcohol, black layer first, then red with the transfer tape lined up on the banner edge.
Smallest details: lid banner hex recess ≈ 0.6 mm, pinstripe ≈ 0.5 mm. Ask the shop if they can weed 0.5 mm; if not, they can drop the pinstripe on the lid version.
